# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Found a flaw in fullesim.com, the checkout, the account panel, the apps or the provisioning flow? Write to **security@fullesim.com**. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it.

- Encrypt your report with our PGP key: https://fullesim.com/pgp/fullesim.asc. Check its fingerprint against more than one source, such as https://fullesim.com/security.
- Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.
- Our machine-readable contact file is https://fullesim.com/.well-known/security.txt.

We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith and gives us time to fix it.
